# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

findMypet exists to help people and communities respond to lost-pet and found-owner situations. Contributors are expected to keep discussions practical, respectful, and focused on improving the tool.

Expected behavior:

- Be respectful and direct.
- Assume good intent, but address concrete risks clearly.
- Keep feedback tied to code, docs, product behavior, or user safety.
- Be especially careful with privacy, location, contact information, and rescue-related content.

Unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, threats, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Using the project to facilitate scams, spam, or abuse.
- Sharing exploit details for active security issues in public threads.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments, issues, pull requests, or contributions that violate this code of conduct. Repeated or severe violations may lead to being blocked from the project.

## Reporting

For conduct concerns, open a private communication channel with the maintainers if available. For security issues, follow `SECURITY.md`.
